Hands-free

Turn on Hands-free in the top bar and just say my name (add "hey" if you like), then your request. Right after I answer you can keep talking for a few seconds without saying it. Automatic uses the wake word model on this computer when there is one: it listens for the name in real time and nothing is uploaded until it has heard it. Otherwise it uses your browser's own speech service (Chrome and Edge send audio to their cloud for it, Safari uses Apple's) and switches to ElevenLabs if that isn't reachable, for example in Brave or the desktop app. With ElevenLabs, only short clips of someone speaking are sent, and it pauses if the room is noisy. It is off until you switch it on, and I ignore everything while I'm talking.
